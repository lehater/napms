# Stakeholder Evidence Synthesis

1. State the synthesis question.
2. Load only current evidence relevant to it; do not preload old chats/repository history as normal evidence.
3. Separate observation from interpretation and distinguish independent corroboration from copies/summaries.
4. Group equivalent observations without erasing materially different actors, constraints or contradictions.
5. Produce candidates only: problem themes, journeys/use cases, requirements, capability clues or boundary hypotheses.
6. Use `docs/canonical-graph.yaml` to identify the owner that would accept each candidate.
7. Persist only conclusions that are actually accepted in that owner; discard working clustering after use.

Evidence volume is not acceptance authority. A stakeholder-proposed solution may reveal a need without becoming a requirement. Boundary clues do not themselves establish a Bounded Context.
